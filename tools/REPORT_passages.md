## Manual QA (2026-09-26, author self-check; no external QA round yet)

- **Authoring.** 60 MSA passages, 20 per level, written by Claude from the
  scaffolding in `tools/passages_gen/` (`make.py` writes
  `tools/passages_src.json`, which is the authoritative source). The rules
  block matches persian's. Not native-reviewed.
- **Questions.** 255 questions (mc 126, tf 129). Every question's `words` are
  linked in its `sentence`. No true/false item shares a 5-word run with the
  passage. No correct option appears in its passage title. Questions about a
  first-person narrator use the narrator's name or الكاتب. Correct mc
  options that repeat 4+ words of a sentence: A1 0/40, A2 0/42, B1 2/44.
  The correct option is the unique longest in A1 4/40, A2 6/42, B1 9/44
  (15% overall). The builder's verbatim-key self-check: A1 13/40, A2 4/42,
  B1 3/44.
- **Balance.** Correct mc answers sit at positions 0/1/2/3 as 32/32/31/31
  (`make.py` rotates the key). True/false answers are 58 true and 71 false
  (A1 19/25, A2 20/24, B1 19/22).
- **Out-of-pack lemmas.** None outside declared names.
- **Names.** Arabic has no capitals, so declared names are skipped only
  through `oop` ("name") and the Arabic `passage_retag` hook (a name behind a
  proclitic, وسلمى/لرنا, is retagged to the name). A declared name that is
  also a pack word (كريم, عمر, حسن, جمال) still links to that word, so
  the passages use names that are no pack word.
- **Authoring conventions (tagger limits, avoided in the text).** Bound
  compounds at B1 (سأل عن, في البداية, انتقل إلى) count as B1 words, so
  A1/A2 texts avoid them. Elatives (أرخص, أغلى, أسهل) are their own
  lemmas and often out of pack. Clock times use digits (الساعة 11). A
  weekday after يوم links the day name (يوم السبت).
